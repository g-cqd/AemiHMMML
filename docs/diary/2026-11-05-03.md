# 2026-11-05: AemiHMMML on aemi's primitives

aemi took the parsing primitives of issue #5 (aemi ADR 0031, commits a71cf06, 87b25af, b52b6e2), and AemiHMMML dropped its copies:
`ByteScan`, `LineIndex`, `SourceLocation`, its key table and hashing, its date parser and its float reader (ADR-0010's
amendment). The public `TOMLDateTime` is now `RFC3339DateTime`; `Diagnostic.located(in:)` takes `AemiText.LineIndex`.

The first measurement after the move was a regression on the numeric file (canada.toml 51 instructions per byte against 42):
aemi's decimal scanner did zero-aware bookkeeping on every digit. Fixed in aemi (b52b6e2), not here, so every consumer gets it;
after it: canada 36, cargo-lock 38, twitter 50, all below what AemiTOML had with its own copies, and still ahead of TOMLDecoder
(47, 50, 63). toml-test passes as before.
